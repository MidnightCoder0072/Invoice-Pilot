Delete Your InvoicePilot Account

You can request deletion of your InvoicePilot account and the personal data associated with it.

How to request deletion

Email eclipsemoon0072@gmail.com from the email address associated with your InvoicePilot account.

Include the subject line InvoicePilot Account Deletion Request and tell us that you want your account and associated data deleted.

What will be deleted?

After your request is verified and processed, we intend to delete:

  • Your InvoicePilot account.
  • Client records stored in your account.
  • Invoices and related invoice data stored in your account.
  • Account settings and associated personal data stored by InvoicePilot.

Processing and retention

We will verify your request and aim to process it within 30 days. If any information must be retained to meet a legal obligation, we will retain only what is required for that purpose and for the legally required period.

Need help?

Contact eclipsemoon0072@gmail.com if you have questions about account or data deletion.
