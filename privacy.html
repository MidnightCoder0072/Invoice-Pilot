InvoicePilot Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 October 2026

1. Introduction

InvoicePilot is an invoicing application that helps users manage clients, create and edit invoices, calculate VAT, track payment statuses, and generate and share invoice PDFs.

This policy explains how InvoicePilot collects, uses, stores, and shares information.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account information: email address and profile name.
  • Client information: details entered about clients.
  • Invoice information: invoice numbers, dates, items, amounts, VAT rates, currency, and payment status.
  • Preferences: selected currency and application settings.
  • Support communications: information provided when contacting us.

Please avoid entering unnecessary sensitive personal information into client or invoice fields.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to authenticate accounts, store and retrieve client and invoice records, calculate totals and VAT, generate PDFs, remember preferences, troubleshoot the application, and respond to support requests.

4. Storage and Third-Party Services

InvoicePilot uses Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore for account authentication and cloud storage. Information processed by these services is also subject to applicable Google and Firebase terms and privacy documentation.

When you share an invoice PDF, it is passed to the sharing functionality available on your device. The recipient and any service you choose may process the file under their own privacy practices.

5. Data Sharing

InvoicePilot does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers supporting the application or disclosed where required by law or necessary to address security, fraud, or misuse.

You control whether to share invoice PDFs and who receives them.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Account, client, and invoice information may remain stored while your account is active.

To request deletion of your account and associated personal information, email eclipsemoon0072@gmail.com with the subject “InvoicePilot Account Deletion”. We will verify requests where appropriate and explain any applicable steps or limitations. Some information may need to be retained where required by law.

7. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed by InvoicePilot. Cloud access is intended to be restricted to the authenticated user's own records. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Users are responsible for protecting access to their devices and accounts.

8. Children's Privacy

InvoicePilot is intended for business and invoicing use and is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information directly from children.

9. International Processing

Information may be processed in countries where Google and its service providers operate, subject to applicable safeguards and legal requirements.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as InvoicePilot changes. The latest version will be published at the policy URL linked from Google Play.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning personal information, contact eclipsemoon0072@gmail.com.
