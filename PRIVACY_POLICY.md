# InvoicePilot Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 10 October 2026

## 1. Introduction

InvoicePilot is an invoicing application that helps users manage clients, create and edit invoices, calculate VAT, track invoice payment statuses, and generate and share invoice PDFs.

This Privacy Policy explains how InvoicePilot collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the application.

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the application, InvoicePilot may process the following information:

- **Account information:** your email address and profile name, used for authentication and account identification.
- **Client information:** details you enter about your clients.
- **Invoice information:** invoice numbers, dates, line items, amounts, VAT rates, currency, and payment status.
- **Preferences:** your selected currency and other application settings.
- **Support communications:** information you provide when contacting us about privacy or technical issues.

Please avoid entering unnecessary sensitive personal information into client or invoice fields.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information is used to:

- Create and authenticate user accounts.
- Save and retrieve client and invoice records.
- Calculate invoice totals and VAT.
- Generate and share invoice PDFs at your request.
- Remember application preferences.
- Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the application's functionality.
- Respond to support and privacy requests.

## 4. Storage and Third-Party Services

InvoicePilot uses Firebase services provided by Google, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, to support account authentication and cloud storage.

Information stored through these services is subject to the applicable Google and Firebase terms and privacy documentation.

When you choose to share an invoice PDF, the file is passed to the sharing functionality available on your device. The recipient and any third-party service you choose to use may then process the file under their own privacy practices.

## 5. Data Sharing

InvoicePilot does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that support the application, including Google Firebase. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or when necessary to address security, fraud, or misuse.

You control whether to share invoice PDFs and who receives them.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Account, client, and invoice information may remain stored while your account is active.

To request deletion of your account and associated personal information, contact **eclipsemoon0072@gmail.com** with the subject line "InvoicePilot Account Deletion".

We will verify the request where appropriate and explain any steps or limitations that apply. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate recordkeeping obligations.

## 7. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed by InvoicePilot. Cloud access is intended to be restricted to the authenticated user's own records. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for protecting access to your device and account.

## 8. Children's Privacy

InvoicePilot is intended for business and invoicing use and is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information directly from children.

## 9. International Processing

Information may be processed in countries where Google and its service providers operate, subject to the safeguards and requirements applicable to those services.

This Privacy Policy may be updated as InvoicePilot changes. The latest version will be published at the policy URL linked from the Google Play listing.
